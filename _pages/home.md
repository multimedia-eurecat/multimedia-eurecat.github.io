    <h2>Our research groups.</h2>

  <div class="home-team-grid">
    <article class="team-card team-card-audio">
      <span class="team-number">01 / Audio</span>
      <h3>Spatial and intelligent audio</h3>
      <p class="group-summary">The Audio Group develops technologies for spatial audio, immersive listening, perceptual audio enhancement, and intelligent audio analysis and generation.</p>
      <details class="group-details">
        <summary>
          <span class="details-label">More information <span class="details-arrow" aria-hidden="true">↓</span></span>
        </summary>
        <div class="group-details-content">
          <p>The group has a long trajectory in immersive sound technologies as a pioneer in 3D audio and founder of the spin-off ImmSound, later acquired by Dolby Laboratories.</p>
          <p>Building on this foundation, the group continues advancing research in 3D spatial audio, virtual acoustic environments, sound scene simulation, and audio technologies for immersive and extended reality experiences. Recent work also integrates machine learning and multimodal AI into audio technologies, addressing audiology and hearing-related applications, XR communication, acoustic heritage reconstruction, and immersive media production.</p>
          <a class="group-page-link" href="{{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/team">View the team <span aria-hidden="true">→</span></a>
        </div>
      </details>
    </article>

    <article class="team-card team-card-image">
      <span class="team-number">02 / Image</span>
      <h3>Computer vision and generative imaging</h3>
      <p class="group-summary">The Image Group develops computer vision and AI methods for understanding, generating, and reconstructing visual content.</p>
      <details class="group-details">
        <summary>
          <span class="details-label">More information <span class="details-arrow" aria-hidden="true">↓</span></span>
        </summary>
        <div class="group-details-content">
          <p>With more than 12 years of experience, the group develops advanced computer vision and AI solutions for public and industrial R&D projects. Originating in FX post-production for the creative industries, its work has expanded to areas such as human behaviour analysis in retail, medical and satellite image enhancement and synthesis, 3D reconstruction for industrial and cultural heritage applications, or intelligent assistants for audiovisual production.</p>
          <p>Its expertise includes multimodal image and video understanding, generative and forecasting models, neural rendering and 3D reconstruction, avatars and human digitisation, and multimodal affective computing.</p>
          <a class="group-page-link" href="{{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/team">View the team <span aria-hidden="true">→</span></a>
        </div>
      </details>
    </article>

    <article class="team-card team-card-visualization">
      <span class="team-number">03 / Visualization</span>
      <h3>Immersive visualization and interaction</h3>
      <p class="group-summary">The Visualization Group develops immersive and interactive technologies for understanding and exploring complex information.</p>
      <details class="group-details">
        <summary>
          <span class="details-label">More information <span class="details-arrow" aria-hidden="true">↓</span></span>
        </summary>
        <div class="group-details-content">
          <p>The group combines extended reality, visual analytics, and digital twins to integrate geolocated information, industrial data, and digital assets into intuitive interfaces.</p>
          <p>Its solutions cover XR headsets, smart glasses, holographic displays, and desktop environments, with the aim of improving user experience and decision-making through advanced visualization and interaction technologies.</p>
          <a class="group-page-link" href="{{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/team">View the team <span aria-hidden="true">→</span></a>
        </div>
      </details>
    </article>
